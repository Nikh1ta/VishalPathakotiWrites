The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 17 — Nandu

They met Nandu later. She listened to everything. The wedding. The Jungle Stay. The pool. Vaishnavi. The hotel. Rudraksha. Mayuri. The basement. The forest. The cave. The pendant. The fire. Everything.

Nandu cried.

Vishwa watched silently. He remembered the wedding. The rain. The farmhouse. The infinity pool. The strange feeling that had followed them. He finally understood. The story had started long before the hotel. Perhaps—it had started the moment they entered the jungle. Perhaps even earlier.

Nandu wiped her tears.

"Why did this happen to us?"

Nobody answered.

Vishwa looked down.

Because there was no answer.

Not yet.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Reflective):

Sometimes, the most frightening things are not the ghosts you can see, but the questions that refuse to leave. As the group sat together, their memories heavy with loss and mystery, the truth lingered somewhere in the shadows—waiting for the right moment to reveal itself. Some stories don’t end. They just wait for the next chapter.
