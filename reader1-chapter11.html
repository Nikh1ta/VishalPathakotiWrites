The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 11 — The Two Spirits

The Trek group escaped the basement. Everyone was shouting. Nobody knew where to go. The burned building seemed to change around them. Hallways appeared longer. Doors opened and closed. Shadows crossed the walls.

Avi ran past Veera.

"Bro!"

"What?!"

"Exit ekkada?"

Veera pointed.

"Right!"

Avi ran left.

Two seconds later—he returned.

"Wrong Right!"

Veera shouted.

"How can Right be wrong?!"

Avi pointed.

"Ee time lo comedy endhuk bayya? I meant—it’s a Dead end!"

Veera closed his eyes.

Raj ran past them.

"Ghosts ki architecture kuda nachindi."

Avi stopped.

"Bro!"

Raj looked back.

"Enti?"

"Adi comedy aa?"

"Maybe."

"Okay."

Avi started running again.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

When the building itself seems lost, even Google Maps can’t save you. All you can do is run, hope the laughter drowns out the fear, and pray the ghosts aren’t good with directions.

Rudraksha gathered everyone in the protected room. He drew a circle with ash. Salt was placed around the entrance. The chanting began. The walls shook. Vaishnavi was still unconscious. Her body lay on the floor.

Vishwa watched her.

"Aame inka possessed aa?"

Rudraksha nodded.

Vishwa looked toward the basement.

"Mayuri ki em ayyindi?"

Rudraksha's face darkened.

"She has merged."

Vishwa swallowed.

"Jungle Spirit tho?"

Rudraksha nodded.

"Two spirits."

Vishwa looked at Vaishnavi.

"Vaishnavi?"

Rudraksha looked at her.

"The spirit used her as a door."

Vishwa's eyes widened.

"Door?"

Rudraksha nodded.

"She was the host."

Vishwa slowly understood. The pool. The possession. The hotel. The basement. Everything was connected. The Jungle Spirit had entered Vaishnavi at 3 AM. Then it used Vaishnavi to reach Mayuri. And Mayuri—had been waiting for twenty years. The merge had made the entity stronger. Much stronger.

Rudraksha finally spoke.

"We cannot stop her here."

Veera stepped forward.

"Then where?"

Rudraksha looked at all of them.

"Chevella."

Silence.

Vishwa frowned.

"Chevella?"

Rudraksha nodded.

"There is a forest."

Avi raised his hand.

Everyone looked at him.

Rudraksha sighed.

"Enti?"

"Forest ante... trees untaya?"

Everyone stared at him.

Avi looked confused.

"Just asking."

Raj looked at him.

"Forest lo trees undadam shocking kaadu."

He paused.

"Avi questions shocking."

Avi stared.

"Enduku nannu target chestunnaru?"

Rudraksha continued.

"Inside the forest is a cave."

Vishwa listened.

"Inside the cave is a pendant."

"Pendant?"

Rudraksha nodded.

"That pendant contains the power connected to her."

Vishwa looked at Vaishnavi.

"What do we do with it?"

Rudraksha's voice became serious.

"Bring it out."

"And?"

"Place it at a particular spot."

Vishwa waited.

Rudraksha continued.

"Break it with a black stone."

Veera frowned.

"Any black stone?"

Rudraksha shook his head.

"One particular black stone."

Vishwa looked at the group.

"Where do we find it?"

Rudraksha looked at him.

"That is the problem."

Avi slowly raised his hand.

"Sir."

Rudraksha looked at him.

"Can we use black stone from kitchen?"

Everyone stared.

Avi defended himself.

"Em? Black stone ante adhi kuda black stone ey ga."

Veera: "adhe black stone tho ninnu kotti champestham chepthunna…"

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

If only exorcisms could be solved with kitchen supplies, the world would be a safer—and slightly weirder—place. But as the night pressed in, the group realized they’d have to face the darkness on its own territory, armed with nothing but hope, courage, and a very specific rock.
