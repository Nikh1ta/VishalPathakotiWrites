The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 12 — The Chevella Mission

12:30 AM. Chevella forest. The road was empty. The forest stood ahead like a wall. The group had divided. Most of the Trek members remained near the road. Only four went inside: Vishwa, Veera, Srikanth, and Rudraksha.

Avi immediately stepped forward.

"I'm coming."

Vishwa stared.

"No."

"Why?"

"Because you're scared."

Avi looked offended.

"Nenaa?"

"Yes."

"Scared aa?"

"Yes."

Avi pointed toward the forest.

"Ante adhi….."

Vishwa blinked.

"Emaindhi?"

Avi nodded.

"Bhayam esthundhi anko…."

Veera laughed.

Avi continued.

"But coming."

Raj stood near the vehicle.

Vishwa looked at him.

"Nuvvu?"

Raj shook his head.

"Forest lo naaku signal dorakadu."

Avi laughed.

"Adi nee reason aa?"

Raj nodded.

"Priorities."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Some people fear ghosts, some fear the dark, and some just fear missing a phone notification. In the Chevella forest, everyone had their own priorities—and none of them involved common sense.

The group started walking. The deeper they went—the darker it became. The electronics stopped working. Phones died. Torches stopped. The only light came from a burning stick.

Avi stared at it.

"Bro..."

Veera looked at him.

"Enti?"

"Human civilization ki centuries progress ayyi..."

He pointed at the stick.

"Finally ikkada malli firewood stage ki vacham."

Srikanth laughed.

Vishwa shook his head.

"Shut up and walk."

The forest was silent. Too silent.

Then—

Crunch. Crunch. Crunch.

Footsteps.

Vishwa stopped.

Everyone stopped.

Another step.

Behind them.

Vishwa turned.

Nothing.

Another step.

The footprints continued.

The group moved.

Vishwa got suspicious about the footsteps, so he and the group stopped. But there were two sets of footprints ahead of them. Veera did a quick head count, and five members were present.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Five friends, five sets of footprints. So... who—or what—had already walked ahead?

Veera, scared: "Evvar ayina mundhuki elli return occharu aa?"

Everyone, scared: "Ledhu."

Veera, scared: "Nijam cheppandi, no jokes please."

Vishwa, scared and stuttering: "Ippu... Ippudu joke chese situation lo manam unnam aa Veera..."

Avi, scared: "Itlanti appude physical ga fitness chupiyali anpisthadhi..."

Everyone: "Ante...?"

Avi: "Lag oddhu, let’s RUNNNN!"

The footsteps became faster. Then—they began running around them. Left. Right. Behind. Above.

Vishwa turned.

Nothing.

Whispers filled the trees.

"Vishwa..."

He froze.

It was Vaishnavi's voice.

"Vishwa..."

Avi whispered.

"Bro."

"What?"

"Turn avvaku."

Vishwa stared forward.

"Why?"

"Horror movies lo turn ayithe problem."

Vishwa nodded.

"Correct ey."

Then Avi heard another voice.

"Avi..."

Avi froze.

He slowly turned.

Vishwa grabbed him.

"Don't!"

Avi whispered.

"Ghost na peru kuda nerchukundi bayya."

Vishwa said nothing.

Avi looked terrified.

"Famous ayipoyanu."

Vishwa: "Itlanti situation lo famous ayithene problem anni dhayalu nee deggarike osthai."

Avi looked around, SCARED.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

In a forest where even the trees seemed to whisper your name, being famous meant you were next on the ghost list.

The forest seemed endless. Then Rudraksha pointed ahead.

The cave.

A massive black opening between rocks. Cold air came from inside.

Rudraksha stopped.

"Many traps."

Avi looked inside.

"Natural?"

Rudraksha shook his head.

"Human-made."

Avi immediately stepped backward.

"Ee Manushulaki vere pane ledhu, edho okkati chesthane untaru, humans are worse than ghosts I say."

Raj's voice suddenly came from behind them.

"Finally correct statement."

Everyone screamed.

Vishwa turned.

"Raj!"

Raj stood there calmly.

Avi pointed.

"Ayya Raaju, nuvvu ekkadi nunchi vachav?!"

Raj shrugged.

"Shortcut."

Avi stared.

"Forest madhyalo?"

"Avunu."

"Alone?"

"Avunu."

"How?"

Raj looked at the cave.

"Wrong question."

Avi frowned.

"Right question enti?"

Raj pointed into the darkness.

"Why am I still alive?"

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Raj’s inner voice: "Phone signal kosam elthunte by mistake lopalki occhi book ayya ani thelsthe cheap look istharu kodukulu."

Nobody laughed. Because it was actually terrifying.
