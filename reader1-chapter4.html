The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 4 — The Story of Ananya

The group stopped at the Jungle Stay again before leaving the area completely. The rain had become heavier. The road ahead was difficult to travel. So they decided to stay one more night. Nobody complained. The farmhouse was large enough for everyone. The weather was beautiful. And the group was tired.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Evening crept in with a cold fog and the kind of rain that felt like it would never stop. The farmhouse glowed with laughter and light, but the jungle outside pressed closer, hiding secrets behind every tree. For one night, the world was just friends, food, and stories—while the storm outside grew stronger, and something in the dark waited for the right moment.

That evening, everyone gathered together. Food was placed on the table. Tea was served. Vishwa sat quietly with a drink in his hand.

Vaishnavi stared at him.

"Vishwa."

"Hmm?"

"Seriously?"

Vishwa looked at the bottle.

"Em problem?"

"Padnalugu rojula nunchi ide pani undhi needhi…."

Vishwa shrugged.

"Count chestunnava?"

"Avunu."

"Why?"

"Because nuvvu aapali."

Vishwa smiled faintly.

"Na life lo evaraina nen chesedhi count chesthunnaaru ante ne adhe great achievement."

Vaishnavi's expression changed.

"Vishwa."

He looked away.

"Enti?"

"Drink pakkana pettu."

Vishwa remained silent.

Veera tried to lighten the mood.

"Bro, bottle ni pakkana pedithe table meeda place untadhi."

Vishwa looked at him.

"Nee comedy ki time idi kaadu."

Veera nodded.

"Okay."

Two seconds passed.

Avi leaned forward.

"Naaku oka doubt."

Vaishnavi sighed.

"Enti?"

"Vishwa bottle tho breakup cheyyali ante first bottle ki explanation ivvala?"

The group laughed.

Vishwa stared at him.

Avi continued.

"Because sudden breakup ante bottle emotionally hurt avvachu."

The group laughed harder.

Vishwa shook his head.

"Avi, ninnu evaru pilicharu?"

Avi smiled.

"Food kosam vachanu."

Raj looked at Vishwa's bottle.

"Fourteen days."

He paused.

"Bottle ki Vishwa birthday kuda thelsu untadhi 14 days nundi kalsi unnaru ga."

Avi laughed.

"Andhuke kadha daily celebrate cheskuntunaru iddharu."

Vishwa finally smiled.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

For a moment, the house was filled with laughter, the kind that bounces off walls and makes even the shadows seem harmless. But outside, the jungle pressed closer, and the rain whispered warnings only the night could understand.

Then Vaishnavi's face became serious. She looked at Vishwa.

"Ananya gurinchi cheppali."

Vishwa's smile disappeared. The room became quiet.

"Vaishu..."

Vaishnavi raised her hand.

"Vishwa, nannu cheppanivvu."

Vishwa leaned back.

"Please cheppaku."

Vaishnavi looked at the others.

"Meeru andariki teliyali."

Vishwa stared at her.

"Enduku?"

"Because you're our friend."

Vishwa stared at her.

"idhi vinnaka inka ekkuva bayam vesthundi."

Everyone laughed softly.

Vaishnavi began.

"Ananya..."

The room became quiet.

Years earlier, Vishwa had trusted someone completely. Ananya. She had been one of the few people he allowed close. For a while, everything had seemed normal. Then things changed. Promises were broken. Trust was damaged. People Vishwa believed in disappeared when he needed them. The details were complicated. The pain wasn't. Vaishnavi explained how Vishwa had slowly changed afterward. He became distant. Suspicious. He stopped trusting people easily. He began expecting betrayal before friendship. And every time someone tried to get close—Vishwa found a reason to step away. The fourteen-day drinking phase had started after the memories returned. One drink became two. Two became a habit. And the habit became a shield.

Vishwa listened silently.

Nobody interrupted.

Vaishnavi continued.

"Ananya story ayyaka, Vishwa completely change ayyadu."

Veera looked at Vishwa.

"Bro..."

Vishwa didn't respond.

Vaishnavi looked at him.

"Still hurt?"

Vishwa stared at the bottle.

"Old story."

Vaishnavi: "Still?"

Vishwa: "Maybe."

Silence.

Then Veera slowly pointed at the bottle.

"At least bottle Vishwa ni betray cheyadhu."

Vishwa looked up.

"Enti?"

Veera: "Em ledhu ippatki ayina loyal relationship lo unnav antunna."

The entire room exploded with laughter. Even Vishwa smiled.

Vaishnavi shook her head.

"Idi serious conversation."

Veera nodded.

"Sorry."

Two seconds passed.

Avi raised his hand.

"One important question."

Vaishnavi sighed.

"Enti?"

Avi: "Vishwa bottle tho serious relationship lo unte..."

He looked at Vishwa.

"Wedding ki bottle ni kuda invite chesara?"

Everyone burst into laughter.

Vishwa threw a cushion at him. Avi ducked.

"Rey! Nenu Nik emotional support undali ani adiga!"

Raj looked at Avi.

"Bottle best man."

Avi laughed.

"Perfect!"

Vishwa shook his head.

"Meeru andaroo mental."

Raj nodded.

"Group membership requirement antaru idhi."

Avi: "Ushhh sare Nik okkadke occhu Raj English (sarcasm)"

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

For a while—everything felt normal. Friends. Rain. Drinks. Jokes. Memories. The kind of night that makes you believe nothing bad could ever happen.

But outside, the rain fell harder. Shadows stretched beneath the doors. Nobody knew that this would be the last normal evening they would ever have together.

Because at exactly 3 AM—Vaishnavi would walk toward the pool.

And the Jungle Water Spirit would be waiting.
