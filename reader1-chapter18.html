The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 18 — The Message in Ash

Before disappearing, Rudraksha had left one final message. Written on the wall. In ash. Five words.

SHE ESCAPED.

SHE WILL ALWAYS BE WATCHING US.

Vishwa stared at the message.

Avi stood beside him.

"Sir..."

Vishwa looked at him.

"Enti?"

"One thing."

"What?"

"Why does every ghost have personal interest in us?"

Vishwa didn't answer.

Nataraj looked at the wall.

"Because bad luck has GPS."

Avi slowly nodded.

"That explains everything."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Some messages are warnings. Others are promises. As the words in ash faded into the wall, the group realized that escape was only an illusion. Once a spirit marks you, their watch never ends—and neither does the story.
