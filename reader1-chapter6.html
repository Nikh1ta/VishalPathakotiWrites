The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 6 — The Hotel

The next morning, nobody understood what had happened. Vaishnavi was found near the pool. She claimed she remembered nothing. Vishwa asked questions. She gave short answers. She looked tired. But something was different. Very different.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Morning brought a strange quiet, as if the rain had washed away more than just mud. The farmhouse felt colder. The group packed up with uneasy glances, pretending everything was normal. But everyone knew—something had changed in Vaishnavi's eyes, and nobody dared mention it out loud.

The group decided to leave. The weather worsened. Roads became difficult. Eventually, they were forced to stop at a hotel. It was old. Uncomfortable. And directly beside it stood a burned six-story building. Its walls were black. Its windows were broken. Yet people still lived nearby. The locals avoided looking at it.

One man quietly warned them.

"Dhayalani odhilistharu anta."

Vishwa stared.

"Ante...?"

Veera answered.

"Akkada Dhayyalu untai antunnaru indirectly."

Vishwa looked at the building.

"Nice."

Veera nodded.

"Very nice."

Vishwa picked up his luggage.

"Hotel change cheddam."

Veera shook his head.

"Money?"

Vishwa stopped.

"Okay."

He looked at the burned building again.

"Ghosts tho compromise etla avvalo kanpisthe."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

If haunted hotels had a rating system, this place would have earned six out of six skeletons. But when you’re broke and tired, even ghostly neighbors become cheaper than a room upgrade.

That evening, Vaishnavi sat downstairs with Vishwa. The others were upstairs. They were talking about Nandu. About the wedding. About the strange trip.

Then Vishwa noticed Vaishnavi wasn't listening.

"Vaishu?"

She stared at him.

No response.

"Vaishu?"

Her head slowly turned.

Vishwa smiled nervously.

"Em ayyindi?"

Vaishnavi looked directly at him.

"Nandu evaru?"

Vishwa frowned.

"Enti?"

She blinked.

"Nandu evaru?"

Vishwa's smile disappeared.

"Vaishu?"

She tilted her head.

"Vaishu evaru?"

The room became silent.

Vishwa stood.

"Enti comedy aa..."

Vaishnavi smiled.

But it wasn't her smile.

Her eyes darkened.

Her mouth twisted.

Vishwa tried to move.

Nothing.

His body froze.

He couldn't breathe.

He couldn't shout.

Vaishnavi leaned closer. Her voice changed. Something else spoke through her.

Then—the door opened.

A figure walked in from the direction of the burned building. The person approached Vaishnavi, placed a hand on her head.

Vaishnavi collapsed.

Vishwa's body suddenly released.

He fell backward.

"Ayyayyo!"

The figure looked at him.

Vishwa pointed at Vaishnavi.

"Aame normal ga ledu!"

The figure picked up Vaishnavi and walked toward the burned building.

Vishwa followed.

Because apparently—that was the worst decision available.

And Vishwa had chosen it.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Sometimes, the scariest thing isn't the ghostly building next door—it's your own curiosity dragging you straight into the nightmare. But for Vishwa, when the night calls, he answers. Even if it means following a stranger, a possessed friend, and all his regrets into the darkness.
