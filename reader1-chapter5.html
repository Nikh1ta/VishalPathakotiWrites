The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 5 — 3:00 AM

At 2:57 AM, the farmhouse was silent. Everyone was asleep. The rain had slowed. The jungle was covered in mist. Vishwa slept deeply. His fever had begun sometime after midnight. The bottle he had been drinking from earlier stood untouched beside his bed.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Night pressed in from every direction, thickening the shadows and muffling the world in a blanket of uneasy silence. The jungle breathed, the rain hiding secrets in the mist. In the darkness, time seemed to bend—waiting for something to break the stillness.

Vaishnavi was awake. She didn't know why. She sat up. The room was dark. Her eyes moved toward the window. Something was calling her. Not with words. With water.

She stood. Walked toward the door. Opened it. The hallway was empty. Vaishnavi stepped outside. The farmhouse was silent. She walked toward the pool. Each step became slower. The air grew colder.

At 3:00 AM—she reached the water.

The pool was perfectly still.

Vaishnavi stood at the edge.

She looked down.

Her reflection stared back.

Then—the reflection smiled.

Vaishnavi did not.

She stepped backward.

The water moved.

A ripple.

Then another.

A hand emerged.

Vaishnavi froze.

The hand grabbed her ankle.

She screamed.

The sound vanished into the rain.

The water pulled.

Vaishnavi fell into the pool.

The world went black.

Deep beneath the water—something opened its eyes. And something ancient entered her.

Vaishnavi's body stopped moving.

Then—her eyes opened.

They were black.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

The Jungle Water Spirit had found a host. The surface of the pool stilled, as if nothing had ever happened. The farmhouse slept on, the rain whispering over the roof, and beneath it all, the first night had begun.
