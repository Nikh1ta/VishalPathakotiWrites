The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 15 — The Return

3 AM. The burned building. Rain. Fog. Silence. The group returned. Rudraksha carried the bottle. Vishwa was exhausted. Veera was wounded. Srikanth could barely walk. Avi was somehow still talking.

"Guys..."

Nobody answered.

Avi continued.

"I think after this mission..."

Vishwa sighed.

"Enti?"

"Vacation ki beach veldham."

Everyone stopped.

Vishwa looked at him.

"Beach?"

"Avunu."

Veera asked.

"Enduku?"

Avi thought.

"Water spirits unte?"

Everyone stared.

Avi quickly corrected himself.

"Okay. Mountain."

Nataraj looked at him.

"Mountain lo ghosts untayi."

Avi nodded.

"Then home."

Vishwa smiled.

"Best idea."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

When you’ve outrun ghosts, spirits, and human eaters, even a haunted house sounds safer than a vacation.

They entered the building.

Voice Over (Jump-Scare Style):

But the real terror always waits when you least expect it.

And then—

SHUNK.

A blade entered Vishwa’s side.

Vishwa’s eyes widened.

He slowly turned.

Vaishnavi.

Standing behind him.

Her eyes were completely black.

Her face was smiling.

Vishwa stared.

"Vaishu..."

She pulled the blade free.

Vishwa collapsed.

Avi screamed.

"Vaishnavi!"

Vaishnavi looked at him.

Avi froze.

She smiled.

Avi slowly stepped backward.

"Okay."

Nataraj stood behind him.

"Wrong time."

Avi nodded.

"Correct."

Vishwa fell.

Everything went dark.

Voice Over (Scary, Sudden):

When the monster wears a friend’s face, trust is the first thing to die. In that moment, the darkness finally caught up.
