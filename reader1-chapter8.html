The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 8 — The Escape

The building exploded into chaos. Doors opened. Ghosts screamed. Shadows moved through the corridors. Vaishnavi's body jerked violently. The spirit inside her had awakened. An assistant came running.

"Aame escape ayyindi!"

Vishwa stared.

"Evaru?"

The assistant pointed.

Vaishnavi was gone.

Vishwa looked at Veera.

"Veera?"

"Enti?"

Vishwa: "Enduku andharu parigedutunnaru?"

Veera looked behind him.

A shadow crossed the wall.

"Endhuko nak ardham ayindhi mana kuda adhe chesthe better…."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Sometimes, all it takes is one shadow on the wall to remind you that courage is overrated. When ghosts start running, smart people follow.

They ran. The Trek group scattered. Everyone was shouting.

Vishwa shouted.

"Andaru kalisi undandi!"

Nobody listened.

One by one, they disappeared.

Vishwa stopped.

Silence.

"Guys?"

Nothing.

"Very good."

He looked around.

"Friendship." (sarcastic tone)

He was alone.

Then—a scream came from above.

Vishwa looked at the staircase.

Terrace.

Rudraksha's warning echoed in his mind: Do not go.

Vishwa swallowed.

"Manaki endhuku le ivvani ellipodham…."

Another scream.

Vishwa closed his eyes.

"Actually…Vaishu undochu emo paina..."

He climbed the stairs. The terrace was covered in fog. Shapes surrounded him. Dozens of spirits. They moved toward him. He backed away.

"Vaishu? Vaishnavi nuvve na?..."

They attacked.

Vishwa was thrown against the wall.

He screamed.

"Rudraksha!"

A hand grabbed him.

He was pulled backward.

Rudraksha.

Vishwa collapsed.

He looked at him.

"Sir!"

Rudraksha dragged him down the stairs.

Vishwa pointed upward.

"Never again."

Rudraksha pointed downward.

Basement.

Vishwa's face changed.

"No."

Rudraksha nodded.

"Sir, meeru nannu save chesaru dhaniki I’m really grateful."

Another nod.

"But atla ani cheppu malla naa life risk lo pettodhu thappu..."

Rudraksha didn’t respond.

Then he opened the basement door.

Vishwa stared into darkness.

Somewhere below—something laughed.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

The deeper you go, the darker it gets. Vishwa knew he should run, but the night was far from over. In the shadows, laughter echoed—low, hungry, and patient. The real horror had only just begun.
