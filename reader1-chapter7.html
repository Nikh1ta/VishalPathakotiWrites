The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 7 — Rudraksha

The inside of the burned building was colder than outside. The walls were blackened. The ceiling was cracked. Yet strange symbols covered the remaining sections of the building. The man carried Vaishnavi into a room. Vishwa followed.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Every footstep echoed like a warning. The air felt heavy, as if the walls themselves remembered everything. Shadows stretched across the floor, weaving between symbols no one could read. If this was a horror movie, Vishwa thought, he’d be the first to run—yet here he was, still following.

"Meeru aameki enduku help chestunnaru?"

The man didn't answer.

Vishwa looked around.

"Hello?"

Nothing.

"Sir?"

Still nothing.

Vishwa became irritated.

"At least Google Maps lo mee location cheppandi, reviews chustha."

The man pointed toward a corner.

Vishwa turned.

A small child stood there.

Vishwa blinked.

"Hi."

The child waved.

Vishwa waved back.

"Nice."

The child suddenly ran toward him. Fast. Far too fast.

Vishwa froze.

The child's face twisted. The skin turned dark. The mouth opened unnaturally wide.

A ghost.

Vishwa screamed.

"Ayyayyo!"

The ghost slammed into an invisible barrier.

BOOM!

It bounced backward.

The silent man finally moved.

He pointed toward the wall.

A symbol glowed.

Protection.

Vishwa stared.

"5 star rating istha nenu ee location ki ee okka incident tho."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Some hotels offer free WiFi. This one had ghost-proof walls. Vishwa figured that had to count for something.

The man then spoke. His voice was weak. But it was a voice. His name was Rudraksha. He had been silent for twenty years. A mute healer. A survivor. Not the one who possessed Vaishnavi. He treated those affected by dark forces.

The building had once belonged to his family. His ancestors had practiced sorcery. But twenty years earlier—something terrible had happened. The reason was something he refused to explain. The building burned. His ancestors died. And Rudraksha lost his voice. For twenty years—nothing. Until now. Until the basement. Until fear.

Vishwa looked at him.

"Why is this building burned?"

Rudraksha didn't answer.

Vishwa waited.

Nothing.

"Okay."

He pointed at the ceiling.

"Then why are we here?"

Rudraksha pointed toward the corner.

Vishwa stared.

"Again ghost?"

Rudraksha walked away.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

In the heart of the ruined building, every answer raised more questions. Vishwa wondered if he’d survive long enough to regret his curiosity. In the corner, the shadows moved—and the night wasn’t over yet.
