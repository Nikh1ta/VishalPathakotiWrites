The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 14 — The Fire

Vishwa opened his eyes. Heat. Smoke. Fire. He tried to move. Ropes. His hands were tied. His legs were tied. Below him—a fire burned. Around him stood the human eaters. They were chanting.

Vishwa stared.

"Really?"

One of them raised a weapon.

Vishwa looked at the flames.

"Prathisaari nanne first select chesthara?"

No one answered.

Vishwa looked around.

"At least mandhu thagina tarvatha champandi bayya, u should respect a man’s last wish you know..."

Still nothing.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Not every hero gets a dramatic last stand. Some just get tied up above a bonfire, wishing for one last drink and a bit of respect.

He noticed the cloth band around his head. An idea.

Vishwa grabbed it with his teeth.

Pulled.

The band came loose.

He lowered it toward the fire.

The cloth caught flame.

He used it to burn the ropes.

SSSSSS!

The ropes snapped.

His hands were free.

His legs remained tied.

The fire rose.

Vishwa jumped.

He landed hard.

Rolled.

The flames went out.

He grabbed a sword.

Then a sharp metal weapon.

And attacked.

The fight was brutal. Blood. Smoke. Fire. Screams.

Vishwa escaped. He ran deeper into the cave. Snakes crawled across the floor. Bones surrounded the walls. He reached the pedestal. The pendant was there.

Vishwa grabbed it.

Blue light pulsed in his bloody hand.

The human eaters appeared again.

They saw the pendant.

And froze.

Vishwa stared.

They backed away.

Avi, who had somehow finally caught up, stared at them.

"Interesting."

Vishwa looked at him.

"What's interesting?"

"They're scared of the pendant."

Vishwa nodded.

Avi looked at the creatures.

"Good."

He pointed at himself.

"Because nenu kuda vaallaki chala scared."

Raj appeared behind him.

"Mutual fear antaru adhi."

Avi nodded.

"Aa occhindayya English teacher."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

It’s always comforting when even the monsters are scared. Nothing builds confidence like mutual terror.

The four men escaped the cave while Srikanth and Rudraksha were waiting for them outside. But the mission wasn't finished. They needed the black stone. They searched the forest. Nothing. No stone. No answer. No way to break the pendant.

Then Vishwa saw something.

An old broken temple.

He walked inside.

Drip. Drip. Drip.

Water fell from the ceiling.

Vishwa stared.

"Water drops sound from inside the temple means only one thing..."

In the center stood a Sivalingam. Black stone. Ancient. Heavy.

Vishwa stared.

"The black stone."

Rudraksha understood.

The five men lifted it.

Barely.

They placed the Sivalingam against the pendant.

Vishwa prayed.

"Please!."

Avi looked at him.

"God ki direct request."

Vishwa ignored him.

Veera and Srikanth pushed.

Rudraksha chanted.

Vishwa held the pendant.

Then—

CRACKKKKK!

A flash of light.

The pendant shattered.

Golden liquid poured from inside.

Everyone froze.

The liquid glowed.

Rudraksha immediately collected it.

He mixed it with water.

Then poured it into a leather bottle.

Vishwa stared.

"Is it over?"

Rudraksha shook his head.

"No."

Vishwa's heart sank.

"Of course."

Avi sighed.

"Okka problem finish ayithe another problem ready."

Raj nodded.

"Lifetime subscription antaru adhi."

Avi: "Bayya assal ninnu meaning evvar adguthunnaru, prathisari endhuk velu pedthav. Ippud dhini meaning cheppaku English lo bhagodhu."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

In the land of ancient spirits and impossible quests, there’s always another problem waiting. For this group, survival wasn’t about courage or strength—it was about never running out of sarcasm, even when the world was burning.
