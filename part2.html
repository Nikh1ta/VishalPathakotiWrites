Part Two — Beneath the Whispering Trees

The group discovers the pendant was never just a prison for Mayuri’s power. Hidden within its markings is a map—a path winding deeper into the heart of the Chevella forest. Somewhere Rudraksha never dared mention. Somewhere the original sorcerers performed their final, forbidden ritual.

But Mayuri is no longer trapped. She is out there, searching. And this time, she wants them all. Especially Vishwa.

Yet, a darker question lingers: Who is the jungle spirit? Why did it help Mayuri escape? Whose side is it truly on?

When the group returns to the forest, they will not be nine. This time, a shadow will walk beside them. Someone who should have died twenty years ago.

In the tangled darkness, Vishwa will be forced to make an impossible choice: Can he save everyone? And if not—who will he leave behind?
