The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 16 — The Hospital

Vishwa opened his eyes. White ceiling. Beeping machines. Pain. Bandages covered his body. He slowly turned his head. Veera sat beside him.

Vishwa whispered.

"Hospital?"

Veera nodded.

Vishwa closed his eyes.

"Alive?"

"Yes."

"Vaishnavi?"

Veera became silent.

Vishwa opened his eyes.

"What happened?"

Veera explained. Rudraksha had disappeared. Before leaving, he had told them the truth. The pendant had been broken. The golden liquid had trapped only part of the entity. The spirit connected to Vaishnavi had left. But the main entity—had escaped.

Vishwa stared at the ceiling.

"So Mayuri is still alive?"

Veera nodded.

Vishwa sighed.

"Of course."

Avi sat in the corner.

He raised his hand.

"One question."

Vishwa looked at him.

"Enti?"

"Can we change friends?"

Veera threw a pillow at him.

Avi caught it.

"Just asking."

Raj stood near the window. He looked outside. Rain fell against the glass.

He spoke quietly.

"She's not gone."

Everyone turned.

Vishwa stared at him.

"Neekela telusu?"

Raj pointed toward the window.

Wet footprints.

One.

Two.

Three.

They appeared outside.

Leading toward the hospital.

Avi looked at them.

"Okay."

Everyone waited.

Avi swallowed.

"Hospital shift cheddama?"

Nataraj looked at him.

"Ghost ki kuda address telusu."

Avi slowly sat down.

"Then no."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

Hospitals are meant to keep you safe, but some things can slip through any door—especially when you leave them unfinished. As the rain traced patterns on the glass and footprints shimmered in the hallway, the group realized: some spirits never let go. And this story was far from over.
