The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 13 — The Cave

The first trap came immediately.

SWISH!

An arrow shot from the darkness. Srikanth moved too slowly. The arrow struck his shoulder. He fell.

"SRIKANTH!"

Vishwa and Veera pulled him back. Blood spread across his clothes.

Avi stared.

"Okay."

Everyone looked at him.

"Good news."

Veera shouted.

"Em good news?!"

Avi pointed.

"Arrow heart ki tagalledu."

Veera stared.

"Bad news?"

Avi looked at Srikanth.

"Shoulder lo hit ayyindi."

Veera closed his eyes.

"Avi!."

(Irritated)

Avi: "Sorry."

Rudraksha pulled the arrow out from Srikanth’s shoulder. Vishwa tore cloth and wrapped the wound.

"Rest."

Srikanth shook his head.

"No."

Vishwa stared.

"You're injured."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

In horror movies, the injured guy always insists on staying. In real life, it’s even less logical—but far scarier.

Later,

Rudraksha pointed inside.

The pendant.

They had to continue.

Vishwa stepped forward.

"I'll go."

Rudraksha grabbed him.

"No."

Vishwa looked at him.

"Why?"

Rudraksha pointed toward the cave.

More traps.

Vishwa smiled.

"Sir, nen decide ayipoina…."

Avi whispered.

"Hero syndrome thelsa miku? Hero la feel avtharu adhi unte."

Vishwa looked back.

"Enti bayya edho antunav?"

Avi:"Em ledu."

Vishwa entered.

CLICK.

He froze.

WHOOSH!

Another arrow. It struck his arm.

"AH!"

He fell backward.

Everyone ran to him.

Blood dripped down his arm.

Avi stared.

"Okay."

Vishwa glared.

"Don't."

"Nen em anatle kadha bhai."

"Nee mokham chusthe thelsthundhi."

Avi nodded.

"Nee chey inka chala cheptundi."

Vishwa closed his eyes.

"Please."

Veera wrapped his arm.

Then Veera stepped forward.

"Ippud nen ey ga Next."

Vishwa stopped him.

"Jagratha bayya…."

Veera nodded.

He entered the cave. This time—he dodged the arrows. Jumped the spikes. Crossed the pit. The cave became darker.

Then—a sound.

Breathing.

Veera stopped.

Something moved.

Tall figures stepped out of the darkness. Human. But not entirely. Pale skin. Bone necklaces. Long hair. Weapons in their hands. Human eaters.

Veera slowly stepped backward.

One grabbed him.

"HELP!"

Vishwa ran.

A stone blade was raised.

Vishwa grabbed the arrow from his own arm. He charged. The arrow struck the attacker's neck. The cave exploded into chaos. The others attacked. Vishwa fought. Veera fought. Srikanth and Rudraksha screamed from outside.

"Veera ni teesukoni bayatiki randi!"

Vishwa was stabbed. Once. Twice. Three times. Blood soaked his clothes. Veera was slashed across the chest.

Avi stood near the cave entrance. He looked inside. Then looked behind him. Then back inside.

"Guys..."

No one heard.

Avi took one step.

"Guys..."

Still nothing.

He looked at Raj.

Raj was standing beside him.

Raj said one line.

"Late entry."

Avi looked offended.

"Bro, nenu emotionally prepare avuthunna."

Raj nodded.

"Fight mundu emotions awasaram aa ra niku."

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

In caves, there’s no such thing as a safe zone—only places where you die slower.

Vishwa crawled deeper into the cave.

Rudraksha shouted.

"LEFT WALL!"

Vishwa looked.

"Behind the skulls?"

Vishwa reached.

His fingers touched something cold.

The pendant.

A faint blue light appeared.

Vishwa smiled.

"Found it!"

Then—

SLASH.

He was attacked from behind.

Vishwa screamed.

His body collapsed.

The pendant fell.

Blue light filled the cave.

And everything went black...

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful):

When the light goes out, the real nightmares begin. In the heart of the cave, the final battle for every soul was about to start.
