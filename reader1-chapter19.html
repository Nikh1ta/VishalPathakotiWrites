The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 19 — The Last Shadow

Days passed. Life appeared normal again. The group tried to forget. They failed. Vishwa returned home. He stopped talking about the basement. He stopped talking about Mayuri. He stopped talking about the forest. He also tried to stop drinking. For the first time in fourteen days, he left the bottle untouched.

But the dreams returned.

Every night.

The black stone. The pendant. The burned door. And the voice.

"Vishwa..."

Then came the footsteps. Wet mud. Outside his window.

At first—one night.

Then another.

Then every time it rained.

Vishwa would wake. Listen. Footsteps. Slow. Heavy. Coming closer. He would sit up. Wait. Silence. Then—one final step. Right outside the window.

Vishwa would turn.

Nothing.

Until one night—he saw it.

A shadow.

Eight feet tall.

Long hair.

Standing in the rain.

Vishwa couldn't move.

The shadow slowly raised its head.

And looked directly at him.

Vishwa stepped backward.

His phone rang.

He nearly screamed.

He answered.

"Hello?"

Avi's voice came through.

"Bro."

Vishwa swallowed.

"Enti?"

"Padukunava?"

"Yes."

"Good."

Vishwa frowned.

"Why?"

Avi paused.

"Because nenu kuda padukovali."

Vishwa stared.

"Then why did you call?"

Another pause.

"Actually..."

"What?"

"I heard footsteps."

Vishwa's blood ran cold.

"Where?"

"Na house bayata."

Vishwa slowly looked toward his window.

The shadow was gone.

Vishwa realized that the entity was following everyone in the group…

Avi whispered.

"Vishwa..."

"Hmm?"

"Manam aa forest ki vellinappudu..."

"Yes?"

"Something followed us?"

Vishwa looked outside. Rain. Darkness. No shadow.

Then—a woman's voice whispered beside his ear.

"Vishwa..."

He dropped the phone.

Avi's voice screamed through the speaker.

"Vishwa?!"

Vishwa slowly turned.

Nothing.

He looked at the window again.

The glass was covered in condensation.

Something had written a word.

With a finger.

MAYURI.

Vishwa stepped backward.

The lights went out.

Complete darkness.

Then—a final whisper.

"Teruvu."

Vishwa froze.

The same words from his dream. The same door. The same voice.

And somewhere far away—a basement door slowly opened.

THE END

... OR MAYBE NOT?
