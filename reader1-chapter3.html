The Whispers from the Charred Remains

Chapter 3 — The Hand

The wedding ended. The rain continued. People slowly began leaving the farmhouse. Luggage was packed. Vehicles were started. Goodbyes were exchanged. But Vishwa remained unusually quiet. He watched as Nandu stood with her newly married husband. There was tension in the air. Something had changed. Nandu's husband made it clear: the old friendships were over. No more meetings. No more contact. No more returning to the group. The words hung heavily in the rain.

Vishwa looked at Nandu.

She looked away.

Nobody knew what to say.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

The farmhouse emptied, but the air never cleared. The rain flowed like secrets down the windows, and every goodbye felt like a door closing somewhere deep in the woods. If anyone had listened carefully, they might have heard something else—something waiting for its turn to speak.

Then Srinivas appeared beside Hansika. He reached for her hand. She rested her head against his shoulder. Neither spoke. Behind them, the infinity pool rippled. Though there was no wind.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

Love can be a shelter, but some things slip between the cracks—like rain, shadows, and the kind of memories you can’t just dry off with a towel. Behind the couple, the pool moved as if something beneath the surface had finally decided to wake up.

The group finally began their return journey. Vishwa sat quietly. Vaishnavi sat nearby. The others were exhausted. The wedding had taken its toll. Nobody noticed the strange change in Vaishnavi. She was staring out the window. Her reflection looked normal. But behind her reflection—for a fraction of a second—something else appeared. A dark figure. Standing inside the pool.

Vaishnavi blinked.

Gone.

She touched the window.

Cold. Very cold.

"Vishwa?"

He turned.

"Enti?"

Vaishnavi smiled.

"Em ledu."

Vishwa looked at her. She looked completely normal. He turned away. Outside, the jungle disappeared behind them. But something had already followed.

Voice Over (Scary, Suspenseful, and Playful):

The laughter and music faded as the car sped away, but the rain didn’t. Nor did the chill that clung to the glass. Far behind, in the place where water meets shadow, something else had begun its journey. And it wasn’t planning to travel alone.
